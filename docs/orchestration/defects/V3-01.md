# V3-01: Focus retention hides a property after ordinary navigation

Status: **CLOSED by independent RETEST-V3-1**.
Owner: `@itrocks/property-select`. Blocking gate: V3.
Tested base: `080f4e0fe0d310f0a5159df17252f994804892c2` / `0.4.3`;
rebuilt tracking version `0.4.4` has identical product source.

## Reproduction

From `/home/baptiste/itrocks/showtime`, with the documented synthetic database available, run:

```bash
node node_modules/@itrocks/property-select/docs/orchestration/environment/checks/V3-reproduce.mjs
```

The independent driver starts ownership-checked standalone **13874** and application **13875**, launches sandboxed
Chromium and uses trusted clicks. It stops both services in `finally`.
Open `/search-fixture` standalone or `/property-select-invoice` and its add-column pane in the real framework.
With an empty query, click the root `tags` relation. Wait for the breadcrumb to mark `tags` current.

Expected ordinary properties: **`tags.invoices`, `tags.name`**.
Observed: **`tags.invoices`, `tags`**, with the obsolete root `tags` row marked `data-retained` and focused.
The valid `tags.name` terminal is missing. The full V3 driver also reproduces this after a recoverable search error,
Clear and opening `tags`. It fails `T12 navigation after provider failure`: expected count 1, observed 0.
The same ordinary-navigation failure occurs on both interfaces at all four required dimensions.

## Evidence and cause

[Eight independent reproductions](../evidence/V3/reproducer.json),
[full gate failure and read-only DOM state](../evidence/V3/results.json),
[standalone screenshot](../evidence/V3/reproduce-standalone-1280-after.png),
[integrated screenshot](../evidence/V3/reproduce-integrated-1280-after.png).
All reproducer click events are trusted; a 300 ms settled observation excludes a transient loading explanation.

In `src/front/property-select.ts`, `render()` treats a focused row absent from new results as retained, including
`search.status === 'idle'` and a changed navigation context. It removes the last valid row to keep the display budget
and appends the focused obsolete row. The focus-retention responsibility needs a context/generation boundary.
No product change was made by the verifier.

## Required correction and independent retest

`FIX-V3-1` must restore complete adjacent properties during ordinary navigation and after clearing/error recovery,
without regressing focused-result retention during enrichment or top-result eviction.
`RETEST-V3-1` must be a fresh verifier distinct from the corrector, replay the eight reproductions and complete the
entire V3 itinerary, including observations not reached after this failure. Keep V3's failure in history.
L4 depends on successful `RETEST-V3-1`. T11 drag remains V5.
