## Diagnosis and required correction

`reflect/src/relation-fact.ts` marks every `TypeType` as relational. `Date` consequently becomes a target.
`framework/src/property-select-provider.ts` forwards that fact and tries to enumerate the target context.
Fix classification at its owner using the framework's scalar/type abstraction; do not put a Date exception in the
selector, hide the HTTP failure, suppress the property, replace the provider or weaken browser-health assertions.
Check nullable/optional scalar dates, actual business classes, inverse facts and typed-path terminal traversal.

`FIX-L3-1` implements the correction and rebuilds affected public outputs. A new verifier performs `RETEST-L3-1`
on its actual commits. V3 and later dependent work remain blocked until the retest passes.
