## Implementation and author observations

`src/search.ts` owns metadata-only search through a narrow context loader. Every normalised word must occur in the
full technical/labelled path. Unicode NFD, mark removal and lowercasing match the installed autocomplete's private
normaliser; its DOM-bound private implementation cannot be imported through a public standalone port.
Default breadth-first budgets are depth 6, 500 contexts, 100 results and 4 concurrent requests per live generation.
Options expose these technical limits without preferences UI. Candidate storage and queued descendants are bounded.
Each depth is reserved in business order, results rank by relative depth/final-word match/business order/full path,
and response arrival does not determine final ranking. No root expansion or business record access occurs.

Navigation and search share the authorised per-instance context cache. Incoming navigation edges seed branch-local
history. Known reciprocal inverse returns occur at most once after N-1/N-N; 1-N, unknown and asymmetric inverse
returns are excluded. Unannotated reverse pairs and repeated unannotated canonical edges stop conservatively.
Distinct sibling paths and distinct self relations are not collapsed by target class. Manual navigation stays valid.
Query/context changes and disposal abort old work and invalidate its generation even for abort-insensitive providers.
Loading, complete, empty, limited and error are distinct; zero matches after a budget is not definitive absence.

The browser view adds search and immediate clear, final-property rows and root-to-parent navigable path segments.
Activations use the actual full path; returning to a remembered prefix keeps descendants and divergence replaces the
suffix. Normal navigation preserves/reruns the query, terminal/object selection preserves context/query.
Keyed rows preserve the exact focused element during enrichment. A focused result evicted by the top-result budget
remains actionable within that budget until focus leaves; final ranking then applies. Plus/main focus identities are
separate. A retained-row focusout refresh waits for the browser's focus transfer to complete.

The reusable synthetic provider supports delayed/reversed responses, explicit inverses, unknowns and a large cyclic
graph. `/search-fixture` is an explicitly allowlisted acceptance fixture, separate from the ordinary standalone demo.
Its read-only observations do not invoke product handlers. Actual inputs are browser fills, clicks and keyboard input.
