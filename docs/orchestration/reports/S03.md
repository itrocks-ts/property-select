## Delivered facts

The public facade exports `Relation`, `relationOf`, `Cardinality`, `RelationDeclaration`, `RelationFact`,
`RelationOptions` and `TargetMultiplicity`. `ReflectProperty.relation` exposes immutable facts without reading
`property.value`, calling a domain getter or constructing a record. Existing reflection accessors retain their API.

`Relation` follows the public decorator/property helper conventions. Its copied, frozen declarations inherit through
prototypes; a child declaration replaces the complete inherited declaration. Omitted cardinality is `unknown`, and
omitted inverse is `undefined`. `relationOf` returns `undefined` when no explicit declaration exists. A namespaced
symbol shared by CJS and ESM permits both public facades to read each other's declarations.

Type facts reuse `property-type`: singular class targets, nullable singular unions, optional properties, collection
and record elements, generic arrays, inherited defaults/types and deferred references. The unique `target` is absent
for ambiguous/unresolved types; readonly `targets` preserves known candidates. `targetMultiplicity` is independent
of opposite-end cardinality: a collection establishes `many`, without inventing an inverse or choosing 1-N over N-N.
The inverse is an explicit logical identity, with no physical SQL/link-table mapping or name inference.
